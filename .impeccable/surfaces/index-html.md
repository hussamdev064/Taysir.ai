# Surface: index.html

Mode: Operate. Audience: Arabic-speaking students and productive families creating a first visual identity. Task: describe an idea, optionally paste a Gemini key, generate, inspect, copy, open the palette in Coolors, browse prompt-based Pinterest logo references, or print its four outputs. Constraints: exact prompt copy and states, native RTL, static three-file structure, no invented proof or additional product sections.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One generous Arabic identity worksheet turns an idea into a printable four-part brand sheet, refusing a generic chatbot conversation or dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Paper-cream ground, Tajawal black type, pastel yellow/pink/mint/lilac sheets, sharp 3px black frames, offset solid shadows, compact labels and circular ink-like color swatches. Dark mode keeps the cards light and legible against an ink ground.
STORY: Read the gently waving promise, let the nearest word answer the pointer with a gentle shift and text-safe pastel color, choose or write an idea, press the single purple action and feel its short shake and pastel splash, wait for one clear status, then copy or print the resulting identity without losing the description.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Right-aligned RTL logo and a left header cluster of yellow API-key and mint theme buttons sit above a centered heavy headline, paper subtitle, three suggestion pills, one large writing field, and a full-width purple CTA. The key button opens a square cream dialog with a masked field and save/clear/close actions; the key stays in page memory only. Mobile stays single-column; desktop constrains the worksheet to readable width. Results replace the initial emptiness below with a two-by-two sheet set: slogan, palette, logo concept, and titled project ideas. The palette card carries a small blue Coolors link; the logo card carries a small pink Pinterest link.
FORM: Pinned centered generator-to-four-card flow; seed: none (pinned) — “Neo-Brutalism (Paperish feel with soft colors)” and the brief's explicit header, hero, input, and card composition. Signature move: controls physically press down/right as their hard shadow shrinks; the main press adds a hard-edged pastel splash, and the headline breathes in a slow whole-word wave while its hovered or touched word colors.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
